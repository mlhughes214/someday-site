Privacy Policy

Effective October 3, 2026

The short version

Someday doesn't collect, store on a server, share or sell any of your information. There's no account, no sign-up, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.

What you enter, and where it stays

The details you give Someday (your age, city, education, GPA, credentials, savings and debt, filing status, household details, career paths, assumptions and saved scenarios) are stored only on your device, in the app's own storage. They are never sent to us or to anyone else.

Every calculation happens on your device. Someday works without an internet connection and doesn't make network requests.

Sharing from the app

If you choose to share a Results summary card, Someday creates an image and hands it to the iOS share sheet. What happens next depends on the app you pick, like Messages or Photos, and is covered by that app's own privacy policy. Someday never shares anything on its own.

Emailing support

If you email dennisandthegang@gmail.com, we receive your email address and whatever you write. We use it only to reply to you and to improve the app. We don't add you to mailing lists or share it with anyone.

Third parties

Someday contains no third-party code for analytics, advertising or tracking.

If you've chosen to share analytics with app developers in your iOS settings, Apple may give us anonymous crash and usage reports. They contain nothing you entered in Someday, and we don't control or receive any other data from Apple.

Your control

Because your information lives only on your phone, you control all of it. Edit your profile any time in More, then Profile. Delete saved scenarios in More, then Saved. Delete the app and everything it stored is removed. We have no copy to delete.

Children

Someday isn't directed to children under 13. It collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top and describe the change in the app's release notes.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email dennisandthegang@gmail.com.

Support Someday produces illustrative educational estimates, not financial, tax, legal, or career advice.
