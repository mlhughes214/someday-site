Common questions

Do I need to set up a profile?

Explore and Path work without one. Results, Goals and Compare need a profile, since they work out taxes and cost of living for you.

Why is my net worth lower than I expected?

Net worth only counts what you actually save, which by default is about a quarter of your spare money. Raise Saving and investing in More, then Assumptions, and it climbs.

Why do future amounts look so big?

All amounts are in future dollars. Pay grows each year and nothing is adjusted for inflation, so a figure 20 years out is in the dollars of that year.

Where is my information stored?

Only on your phone. Someday has no account and makes no network requests. See the Privacy Policy.

How do I delete my information?

Edit or remove things in Profile and Saved, or delete the app to remove everything it stored. We hold no copy.

Is this financial advice?

No. Someday shows illustrative estimates built from public data and named assumptions. Verify anything before acting on it.

Where do the numbers come from?

Tax rules come from the IRS, the Social Security Administration and state tax agencies. Cost of living comes from the MIT Living Wage Calculator, property tax from the US Census Bureau, and pay from the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics and published salary sources, each cited next to its figure in the app.

Privacy Policy Someday produces illustrative educational estimates, not financial, tax, legal, or career advice.
